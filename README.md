# Vlogger Simulator
Vlogger Simulator is a cozy vlogging RPG created for the [Tên cuộc thi] with the theme of "Vietnam - Korea Cultural Crossover."

Step into the shoes of an aspiring female vlogger traveling across the picturesque landscapes of both countries. Frame the perfect shots, record vibrant cultural moments, and grow your online channel. Use your revenue to upgrade your camera equipment, buy new lenses, and unlock breathtaking new landmarks. Experience and share the beautiful blend of Vietnamese and Korean cultures through the lens of your camera!

🌟 About The Game
A cultural travel RPG developed for competition.

📸 Snap & Vlog: Play as a female content creator capturing the breathtaking intersection of Vietnamese and South Korean cultures.

📈 Grow Your Channel: Post your scenic videos online to gain followers and earn revenue.

⚙️ Upgrade & Unlock: Spend your earnings to buy better broadcasting gear and unlock new iconic destinations across VN and KR.
